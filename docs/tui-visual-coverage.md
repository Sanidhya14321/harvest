## Meaning of the states

**Focused proof** means named behavior tests or actual VirtualTerminal interactions passed; it does not certify every state, terminal or backend. **Partial** means a family is mapped and updated but still has unverified cases or failing broader checks. Supporting modules may not paint their own surface. No filename or successful constructor is counted as visual proof.

The previous PNG/VT evidence predates the final Composer, capped-dialog, Activity and revision integration. It is retained with limitations in [the evidence README](tui-visual-implementation-evidence-2026-10-08/README.md). Final verification uses actual VT cell/input contracts without an additional screenshot batch.
